💡 Understanding the Model

The frictionless ramp model assumes no energy loss from surface contact. This simplification gives clean equations: a = g·sin(θ). It works well for very smooth surfaces or quick calculations.

Try toggling assumptions to see what friction and rotation do to the motion!
